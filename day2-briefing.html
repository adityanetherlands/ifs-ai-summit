Deep Dives

What the Hands-On Blocks Are For

The day is designed to move leaders from observation to lived experience: build something, produce something, then decide what this changes for next year.

10:45 – 12:30

Replit: will a working tool into existence

Block 1 is a fast, concrete introduction to AI-assisted software creation, guided by Replit Field Architects. The point is not to become an engineer in two hours; it is to feel how much of the distance between idea and working product has collapsed.

  • Build a small full-stack application with guidance.
  • See how prompts, iteration, and inspection replace long handoffs.
  • Bring back a sharper instinct for what prototypes can now prove.
13:30 – 17:00

Claude Cowork: raise the baseline quality

Block 2 with Anthropic Field Architects Alan Zhang and Sean Hall focuses on high-quality artifacts and functional automation. Explore how AI can create briefs, plans, analyses, process maps, and workflows with a level of quality that lifts the entire organisation.

  • Generate polished artifacts from messy context.
  • Automate an outcome that matters in your function.
  • Build at least one reusable skill for work at IFS.
Throughout the day

Functional rethink: what changes next year?

Throughout the day, keep asking what becomes possible when AI is not a side tool, but part of the operating model. The best output is not just a prototype; it is a new point of view on how your function should work.

  • Identify one workflow that should not stay manual.
  • Notice where quality, speed, and ownership can move together.
  • Translate the day into a practical next-year operating idea.

Before You Board

Day 2 Readiness

The day only works if everyone can build. Please treat the morning logistics as part of the session.

Bring an admin-unlocked laptop and working AI access.

You should be able to log in, install or approve browser-based tooling if needed, access Replit, and use Claude/Cowork without waiting on IT help. If your machine blocks normal setup flows, sort it before the Day 2 transfer.

Admin-unlocked laptop

Bring the machine you can actually work on, with permissions sorted before breakfast ends.

Claude / Cowork access

Confirm you can sign in and create artifacts, skills, or workflows during the Anthropic block.

Replit access

Make sure your account works and your browser can run the Replit environment.

Charger and adapters

Bring your charger, any adapters you depend on, and enough battery to start the day confidently.

Wi-Fi on arrival

Use the 09:00 coffee window to connect to venue Wi-Fi and open the tools before the welcome starts.

One real workflow to test

Arrive with a process, artifact, decision, or customer workflow from your function that AI might change.
